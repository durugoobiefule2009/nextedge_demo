Research

Research Grants

We fund student-led food science research projects that tackle real-world food challenges in local communities. Applications are reviewed by expert panels of food scientists and academics.

Grants of up to ₦500,000 per project
Open to undergraduate and postgraduate students
Expert mentorship throughout the grant period
Publication and dissemination support
Apply Now
Mentorship

Mentorship

Connecting students with experienced food scientists, academics, and industry professionals who provide personalised guidance, career advice, and research direction.

1-on-1 mentorship matching
6-month structured mentorship cycles
Access to mentor network of 50+ professionals
Regular group mentorship sessions

Skill-Building
