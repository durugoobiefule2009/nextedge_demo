Core Architecture

Programmes & Initiatives

Comprehensive academic capacity building, competitive research grants, global mentorship, and scientific publications designed for emerging Nigerian scholars.

Core Pillars

6 Key Pillars of Execution

Capacity Building

1. Institutional Capacity Building Masterclasses

Direct departmental partnerships (e.g., UNIZIK) delivering university masterclasses led by global experts. Features the Masterclass Spotlight Hub with on-demand Zoom session recordings, slide downloads, and view analytics.

Departmental cohorts and faculty partnerships
Global expert speakers & interactive Zoom sessions
On-demand Masterclass Spotlight Hub
Showcase

2. Research Proposal Event & Showcase

Competitive research showcases, live defense video highlights, prize winner awards, professor-led judging panels, and an archive of publishable co-authored scientific papers.

Live defense video highlights and evaluation criteria
Professor-led evaluation panels and prize awards
Co-authored scientific paper publishing support
Research

3. Collaborative Research Initiatives

Ongoing joint scientific investigations, open datasets, and multi-institutional research collaborations between NexEdge scholars, global mentors, and partner universities.

Joint scientific research projects & open datasets
Multi-institutional co-investigation teams
Publication assistance in peer-reviewed journals
Grant Funding

4. Research Grant Application Hub & Funder Gateway

Active seed funding calls, undergraduate micro-grants, conference travel awards, eligibility guidelines, and online submission portals for scholars alongside dedicated Funder channels for sponsors.

Undergraduate micro-grants & conference travel awards
Direct online application submission portal
Dedicated Funder Gateway for alumni & sponsors
Mentorship

5. Global 1-on-1 Mentorship Portal

Selective advisory matching emerging scholars with global researchers (from Oxford, Reading, Uppsala, etc.) for postgraduate applications, career guidance, and thesis development.

Direct 1-on-1 advisory with international researchers
Postgraduate application & scholarship guidance
Thesis methodology and manuscript review
Grassroots Impact

6. Community Projects & Grassroots Impact

Grassroots STEM outreach, local sustainability initiatives, and community engagement metrics across Nigerian university host communities.

Grassroots STEM & research outreach
Local sustainability and food security projects
Measurable community impact metrics
