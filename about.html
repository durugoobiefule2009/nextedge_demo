INSTITUTIONAL PURPOSE

About Us & Verification

NexEdge Research Scheme equips emerging Nigerian scholars alongside leading global experts through university masterclasses, research grants, 1-on-1 mentorship, and publishable scientific outputs.

Our Foundation

Bridging Local Ambition & Global Academic Rigor

NexEdge Research Scheme was established to address key challenges facing emerging Nigerian scholars: limited access to global research networks, insufficient undergraduate research funding, and lack of structured 1-on-1 scientific mentorship.

By bridging local university departments (e.g., UNIZIK) with international scholars from Oxford, Reading, Uppsala, and beyond, NexEdge builds sustainable academic capacity from undergraduate to postgraduate levels.

Our Mission

To equip emerging Nigerian scholars with the practical skills, mentorship, seed funding, and global networks needed to produce world-class, peer-reviewed scientific research.

Our Vision

A vibrant, self-sustaining African research ecosystem that consistently produces globally competitive scholars and evidence-based solutions for regional and global challenges.
