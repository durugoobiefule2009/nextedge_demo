Who We Are

About NextEdge Research Scheme

We are a nonprofit organisation committed to advancing food science research and mentorship for students and early researchers across Africa.

Our Purpose

Empowering the Next Generation of Food Scientists

Founded by passionate food scientists, researchers, and academic advocates, NextEdge Research Scheme was born from a belief that sustainable agricultural solutions begin with empowering local researchers. We connect talented students with the resources, networks, and guidance they need to thrive.

Our scheme provides competitive seed grants, hands-on lab training, and structured 1-on-1 mentorship to build self-sufficient research capacity across Africa's tertiary institutions.

What Drives Us

Our Mission & Story

Our Mission

To empower the next generation of food scientists and researchers through research support, expert mentorship, and independent training that creates real-world self-sufficient impact. We believe every student researcher deserves access to the tools and guidance needed to succeed.

Our Story

Founded by passionate food scientists and researchers, NextEdge has grown into a thriving community of innovators. What began as a small university mentorship circle has evolved into a nationally recognised scheme supporting hundreds of researchers across Nigeria and beyond.
