GLOBAL COLLABORATION

Network & Get Involved

Connect with NexEdge Research Scheme through 6 specialized engagement pathways. Whether you are an undergraduate scholar, a global researcher, a departmental head, or an institutional sponsor, there is a role for you.

6 Pathways to Join

Choose Your Pathway

Pathway 1

1. Members / Student Researchers

Undergraduate and postgraduate scholars seeking masterclasses, seed research grants, thesis guidance, and open toolkit access.

Access to all masterclasses & slide decks
Eligibility for undergraduate seed grants
Pathway 2

2. Mentors / Academic Experts

Professors, postdoctoral researchers, and industry specialists (from Oxford, Reading, Uppsala, etc.) providing 1-on-1 advisory.

Direct 1-on-1 scholar advisory
Guest speaker & judging panel roles
Pathway 3

3. University Departments

Heads of Department and faculty members seeking masterclass co-hosting, student research capacity building, and joint events.

Departmental cohort capacity building
Faculty collaboration & joint defenses
Pathway 4

4. Student Ambassadors

Student leaders coordinating campus awareness, masterclass mobilization, and peer research support at local universities.

Campus leadership & event organization
Official ambassador certification
Pathway 5

5. Volunteers

Enthusiastic individuals assisting with event operations, logistics, media production, and digital community management.

Event management & digital media roles
Volunteer recognition certificate
Pathway 6

6. Donors & Sponsors

Individuals, alumni, foundations, and corporate partners sponsoring research seed grants, travel awards, and student masterclasses.

Direct student grant sponsorship
Named grant awards & impact reporting
